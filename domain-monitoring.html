Frequently asked questions

How do I check a domain for data breaches for free?

Type the domain into the box at the top of this page and press Check my domain. You get the breach count, the number of exposed addresses, and the last-seen date, with no signup. Verifying the domain unlocks the address list and the breach names.

Is my company domain in a data breach?

Run the free check above. A zero result means none of the domain's addresses appear in the 782 breaches we index today. Verify the domain to be alerted the moment that changes.

What does domain exposure mean?

The email addresses on a domain that appear in known breaches, plus whatever leaked alongside them. One exposed address is a phishing target; many with passwords are a credential-stuffing risk for the whole organisation.

How is this different from checking one email?

An email check covers one person. A domain check covers every address on the domain at once, including staff who never checked, and after verification it keeps watching every new breach automatically.

How is this different from Have I Been Pwned's domain search?

Have I Been Pwned charges for domain search by address count. XposedOrNot's domain check and monitoring are free with no size limit, stealer logs included.

Really free? What's the catch?

No catch. XposedOrNot is open source and has run in the open since 2017. The commercial xonPlus products keep the free tier free. Searches are never logged, sold, or shared.

Can someone monitor a domain they don't own?

No. Monitoring only activates after ownership is verified by email link, DNS TXT record, or an HTML tag or file on the site.

What happens to my employee email data?

We store only addresses found in publicly circulating breaches, plus public breach metadata, never passwords. Per-address results appear only after verification, and nothing is sold or shared. Details in the methodology and Transparency Report.

How fast are alerts after a new breach?

Automatic. When a newly indexed breach contains addresses on your verified domain, an email goes to your monitoring address and the breach appears in your dashboard's real-time alerts.

Can I monitor multiple domains?

Yes. Verify each domain you control and manage them all from one dashboard.

Is there an API?

Yes. After verification the dashboard issues an API key for the domain-breaches endpoint, and official connectors exist for Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, and Wazuh. Team access and Slack, Teams, or webhook alerts are in xonEnterprise+.
